# References

## Models

- **MLP:** Rumelhart, Hinton, Williams. *Learning representations by back-propagating errors.* Nature, 1986.
- **KAN:** Liu et al. *KAN: Kolmogorov-Arnold Networks.* 2024. [https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.19756](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.19756)
- **Fair KAN vs MLP:** Yu, Yu, Wang. *KAN or MLP: A Fairer Comparison.* 2024. [https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.16674](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.16674)
- **FourierKAN (variants):** Xu et al. *FourierKAN-GCF.* 2024. [https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.01034](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.01034); Mehrabian et al. *Implicit Neural Representations with Fourier KANs.* 2024. [https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.09323](https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.09323)
- **Data-reuploading QNN:** Pérez-Salinas et al. *Data re-uploading for a universal quantum classifier.* Quantum, 2020. [https://arxiv.org/abs/1907.02085](https://arxiv.org/abs/1907.02085)
- **QKAN / QVAF (edge activations):** Jiang et al. *Quantum Variational Activation Functions Empower Kolmogorov-Arnold Networks.* 2025. [https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.14026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.14026)
- **QuIRK:** Sharma et al. *QuIRK: Quantum-Inspired Re-uploading KAN.* 2025. [https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.08650](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.08650)
